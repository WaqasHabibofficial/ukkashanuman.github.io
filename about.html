About us

Fabio Fantolino is an architect and designer who established his studio in 2001 in Turin, and later expanded with a second location in Milan in 2014. His projects range from architecture to interior design, including residential and hospitality projects. He specialized in product design, working as a designer and art director in collaboration with various furniture companies.
Arising from a strong and recognized culture of custom design, the goal is always to create unique and exclusive solutions not only for private clients but also for brands and exhibitions.
In recent years, he has started working as a yacht designer, collaborating with companies in the nautical industries. All projects have a distinctive identity, shaped by creative sensitivity and a deep understanding of international design.

Over the years, the studio has successfully blended elegance, design, and technique — the essential components of architecture. These elements are daily curated and mixed to achieve results that are both exclusive and exemplary. Every project is characterized by balance, where aesthetics and client requirements harmonize.
